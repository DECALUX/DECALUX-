Chauncey Campanaro

Actor

Born in Rome in 1989, Chauncey Campanaro grew up between Italy and the United States, read Literature with honors at the American University of Paris, and trained at the Lee Strasberg Institute on the Johnny Depp Acting Scholarship and with the Royal Shakespeare Company at the Shakespeare Institute under Ewan Fernie.

He directed the feature L’Homme au Piano, in English, French and Catalan, edited by Mathilde Van de Moortel (Mustang), photographed by Gina Ferrer (Biutiful) and written by Olivia Baes, translator of Duras.

He is six foot five, speaks four languages, and lives in Los Angeles.

Named for Chauncey Gardiner in Being There. Raised in Rome, educated in Paris, trained in New York and Stratford, a feature directed at thirty. He comes into the room with nothing to prove and everything to give. Now, only acting.
